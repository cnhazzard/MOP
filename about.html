About

The Ministry of Pies was started by Chris Hazzard in 2018 as a way for him to loan out his writing services as part of the professional writing team Hazzard & Fontana (www.hazzardampersandfontana.com). In 2026 the Ministry of Pies branched out by working to develop their first free consumer app to be released in December 2026. With any luck this app will be the first of many!
